Reflection

How I made it

I made these in GIMP. For Gru in the forest I cut Gru out of his picture, put him on a new layer over a misty forest path, and changed his colours and brightness so he looked like he was standing in the same light as the background.

What was hard

Cutting around the edges cleanly was the hardest part. If the cutout was rough you could tell straight away that he had been pasted in. Getting his colours to match the misty forest also took a lot of trying.

What went well

Using layers meant I could change one part without wrecking the rest. Once the colours matched, the edit looked a lot more real than I expected.

Next time

I would add a shadow under Gru so he looks like he is actually standing on the path, and spend more time on the edges of the cutout.

Reflection

How I made it

I made three things in Fusion: a room, a duck cutout and a Lego head. Each one started as a 2D sketch, then I extruded it into 3D and added details on top. For the Lego head I used rounded edges to make it look like the real thing.

What was hard

Getting the sizes right was hard. If one sketch was off, everything built on top of it was off too. In the room, lining the furniture up so nothing floated or went through the walls took a while.

What went well

Once I understood sketch then extrude, making new shapes got a lot quicker. I'm happiest with the Lego head because it looks close to a real one.

Next time

I would plan the measurements before I start so I don't have to go back and fix things, and I would add colours and materials so the models look more finished.

GDevelop 5

Paper Cut

A platformer I made where everything looks like it's cut out of black paper. Get to the door at the end of the level.

Controls: left and right arrows to move, space to jump. Press space again in the air to double jump.

Grab the shiny glints for 100 points each. If you hit spikes or fall off you lose 50 and go back to the last flag you touched. The faster you get to the door the more bonus points you get. Press R at the end to play again.

Play full screen

Reflection

How I made it

I made Paper Cut in GDevelop 5. The idea was that everything looks like black paper cut-outs. I used the platformer behaviour for the player, then used events to add the double jump, points for the glints, spikes, checkpoint flags and a time bonus at the door.

What was hard

Bugs were the hardest part. The player's hitbox didn't match the picture, so you could die from spikes you never touched. There was also a bug where the player jumped up a bit when landing. Fixing them meant testing the same jump over and over until I found what was wrong.

What went well

The paper look worked how I wanted, and the checkpoints and points make it feel like a real game. Being able to play it right here on the website is the bit I'm most proud of.

Next time

I would add more levels and some sound effects, and I would test the hitboxes earlier instead of finding the problems at the end.
